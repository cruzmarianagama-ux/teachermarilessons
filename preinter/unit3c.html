Read · Infer · Interpret paraphrasing strategies
Read the conversation
Ben keeps forgetting words. Read how Nina helps him, then do the three exercises.
Lost for words at the airport

Ben: Yesterday was chaotic. First, I couldn’t find that piece of paper which shows what you paid for something.

Nina: Your receipt?

Ben: Yes! Then I went to that place where you can buy medicine because I needed something for a headache.

Nina: The drugstore.

Ben: Exactly. After that, I met the woman who lives in the flat next to mine.

Nina: Your neighbour?

Ben: Right. She was going to the airport too. When we arrived, it was really... you know, the opposite of empty.

Nina: Crowded?

Ben: Yes. And we couldn’t find one of those things which you push with your bags on it.

Nina: A trolley.

Ben: That’s it. Then we went to the building where our flight was leaving from.

Nina: The terminal?

Ben: Exactly!

Exercise 1 – Recover the Missing Words ✓ Done
Complete the summary with ONE word from the conversation.
Exercise 2 – What Does Ben Mean? ✓ Done
Choose the meaning of each explanation from the conversation.
Exercise 3 – Match the Strategy ✓ Done
Match each explanation from the conversation with the strategy Ben uses.

✍️ Exercise 3 – Writing Challenge: Lost for Words ✓ Sent
Write five short explanations without writing the target words. Include one person, one place, one object, one adjective and one activity.
MODEL TEXT
1. It’s somebody who works in a restaurant and prepares the food.
2. It’s somewhere where you can borrow books or study quietly.
3. It’s something which you use when it rains. You hold it over your head.
4. It’s the opposite of dangerous.
5. It’s a kind of holiday activity. You visit famous buildings, museums and interesting places.
Try to use different strategies:
  • ✓ somebody who...
  • ✓ somewhere where...
  • ✓ something which...
  • ✓ the opposite of / similar to / like
  • ✓ a kind of / for example

UseRelative wordExample

thingswhich / thatA receipt is something which shows what you paid.
placeswhereA campsite is somewhere where people stay in tents.
💡 The relative clause gives essential information about the person, thing or place.
