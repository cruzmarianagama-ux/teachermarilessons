Present Simple & Present Continuous
Present Simple – Positive & Negative
SubjectPositiveNegative
I / You / We / Theyworkdon't work
He / She / Itworksdoesn't work
Use present simple for habits, routines, and facts. Key words: always, usually, often, sometimes, never, every day.
Present Simple – Questions
SubjectQuestionShort answer
I / You / We / TheyDo you work?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
He / She / ItDoes she work?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Present Continuous – Positive & Negative
SubjectPositiveNegative
Iam workingam not working
He / She / Itis workingisn't working
You / We / Theyare workingaren't working
Use present continuous for actions happening now or around now. Key words: now, at the moment, today, this week, Look!

Stative verbs (NOT used in continuous): know, like, love, hate, want, need, prefer, understand, believe.
Describing People – Key Questions
QuestionAnswer type
What does she look like?Physical description (tall, slim, dark hair...)
What's she like?Personality (friendly, funny, shy...)
What is she wearing?Clothes right now (a blue dress...)
What does she usually wear?Typical clothes (jeans and a T-shirt)
