Exercise 1 – Read the Text
Read Emma's text and answer the questions below.
🇧🇷 Leia o texto e responda às perguntas.
Hello, my name is Emma and I am from Mexico. I live in Mexico with my parents and we have breakfast together every day. My mom and dad always have coffee for breakfast, but I don't like coffee, so I never have coffee. I usually have milk, chocolate, or cereal with fruit. My mom and dad eat fruit too, but they don't eat fruit in their cereal. For lunch, we all have the same thing: rice, salad, vegetables, and meat. On Saturdays, we usually have fish for lunch. My mom and dad don't drink water with lunch, but I love water. For dinner, my parents usually have dinner together at home, and I have dinner at college. I have a sandwich and juice or water. My favourite food is chocolate, but I don't eat chocolate every day. My favourite drink is water.
