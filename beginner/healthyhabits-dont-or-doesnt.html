Present Simple: Affirmative & Negative
Affirmative – S or No S?
SubjectVerb formExample
I / You / We / Theybase formI drink water. / They play tennis.
He / She / Itverb + -s / -esShe drinks tea. / He goes to the gym.
⚡ Spelling: go → goes  |  watch → watches  |  play → plays  |  eat → eats  |  listen → listens
Negative – Don't or Doesn't?
SubjectAuxiliaryExample
I / You / We / Theydon'tI don't eat junk food.
He / She / Itdoesn'tShe doesn't drink coffee.
⚡ After doesn't, always use the base form — NOT the -s form.
✅ She doesn't eat    ❌ She doesn't eats
Quick Reference
Affirmative ✓Negative ✗
I drink water.I don't drink water.
She eats fruit.She doesn't eat fruit.
They play tennis.They don't play tennis.
He goes to the gym.He doesn't go to the gym.
We listen to music.We don't listen to music.
