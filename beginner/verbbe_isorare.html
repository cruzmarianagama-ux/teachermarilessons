Verb Be – Positive
SubjectVerb beShort formExample
Iam'mI'm Brazilian.
he / she / itis'sShe's at home.
you / we / theyare'reThey're British.
Use is with singular subjects (one person or thing). Use are with plural subjects (more than one) and with you / we / they.
Verb Be – Negative
SubjectNegativeShort formExample
Iam not'm notI'm not French.
he / she / itis notisn'tHe isn't at work.
you / we / theyare notaren'tThey aren't old.
Verb Be – Questions & Short Answers
QuestionShort Answer (Yes)Short Answer (No)
Are they British?Yes, they are.No, they aren't.
Is she at home?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Are you friends?Yes, we are.No, we aren't.
In short answers, never use a short form for YES: say Yes, they are. NOT "Yes, they're."
WH-Questions with Are
Question wordAreSubjectExample answer

This / That / These / Those
NearFar
SingularThisThat
PluralTheseThose
This/These = near you. That/Those = far from you. Use is with this/that. Use are with these/those.
