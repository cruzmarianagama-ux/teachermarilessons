Exercise 3 — What Do You Know About Ryan?
Read the conversation and answer the questions.
Emma: What time do you usually start work, Ryan?
Ryan: At nine. I work in an office from Monday to Friday.
Emma: Do you have lunch at work?
Ryan: No, I don't. I usually go to a café near the office.
Emma: What do you do after work?
Ryan: On Mondays and Wednesdays, I go to the gym. On other days, I usually go home and cook dinner.
Emma: Do you watch TV in the evening?
Ryan: Not very often. I usually listen to music or read.
Emma: What time do you go to bed?
Ryan: At about eleven.

Exercise 1 — A Day in Lucy's Life
Read the text and decide if each sentence is True (T) or False (F).
Lucy is 30 and she works at home. She usually wakes up at 7:00 and has breakfast at 7:30. She drinks coffee in the morning, but she doesn't eat a big breakfast. She starts work at 8:30.

Lucy has lunch at home at about 1:00. She finishes work at 5:00. On Tuesdays and Thursdays, she goes to the gym after work. On other days, she usually goes for a walk.

In the evening, Lucy cooks dinner and sometimes watches YouTube. She doesn't play video games. She usually goes to bed at 11:00.
Exercise 2 — Writing Challenge: A Day in My Life
Write 8–10 sentences about your own routine. Use the guide below.
Model text
I usually wake up at seven o'clock. I have breakfast at home and I drink coffee in the morning. I start work at eight thirty. I usually have lunch at home at one o'clock. After work, I sometimes go for a walk. I watch YouTube in the evening, but I don't play video games. I usually go to bed at eleven o'clock.
  • What time do you wake up?
  • What do you have or drink in the morning?
  • What time do you start work or study?
  • Where do you have lunch?
  • What do you do after work or study?
  • Something you do regularly (I usually / I sometimes...)
  • Something you don't do (I don't...)
  • What time do you usually go to bed?
