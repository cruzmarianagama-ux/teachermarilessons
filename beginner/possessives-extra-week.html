Possessive 's — Singular (one person)
FormExampleSame meaning
name + 'sAnna's bag is red.Her bag is red.
name + 'sLeo's passport is new.His passport is new.
💡 Atenção: usamos 's para mostrar que algo pertence a uma pessoa. Ex: Anna's bag = the bag of Anna.
Possessive ' — Plural (two or more people)
FormExample
plural noun + 'My friends' names are Ben and Tom.
plural noun + 'My parents' passports are new.
💡 Atenção: quando o substantivo já está no plural (friends, parents, sisters), coloque apenas ' depois do s.
my friend's (1 amigo) vs my friends' (2+ amigos)
