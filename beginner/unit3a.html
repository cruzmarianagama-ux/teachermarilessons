A / An · Plural Nouns
🔍 A or An?
RuleExample
a + consonant sounda phone, a watch, a key
an + vowel sound (a,e,i,o,u)an umbrella, an ID card, an email
💡 We use a/an only with singular nouns. We don't use them with plural nouns: bank cards, not a bank cards.
🔢 Plural Nouns
RuleExample
most nouns: + -skey → keys, camera → cameras
-ch, -sh, -ss, -x, -o: + -eswatch → watches
consonant + -y: -y → -iescity → cities, country → countries
irregular / always pluralglasses
