Grammar Bank
Verb Be · Present Simple · Questions
Verb Be – am / is / are
SubjectBeExample
IamI am Brazilian.
you / we / theyareThey are students.
he / she / itisShe is a nurse.
Use be to talk about nationalities, jobs, feelings, and states. Don't use do/does with be.
Present Simple – Affirmative
SubjectVerbExample
I / you / we / theybase formI work from home.
he / she / itverb + sShe works at a hospital.
Add -s (or -es) for he/she/it. The verb watch becomes watches; go becomes goes.
Present Simple – Negative
SubjectNegativeExample
I / you / we / theydon't + verbI don't eat fast food.
he / she / itdoesn't + verbShe doesn't watch TV.
After doesn't, the verb goes back to the base form — no -s: She doesn't work (not doesn't works).
Present Simple – Questions
SubjectQuestionShort Answer
you / we / theyDo you work?Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
he / she / itDoes she work?Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
he / she / it (be)Is she tired?Yes, she is. / No, she isn't.
Use is/are for be questions. Use do/does for all other verbs. Never say Does she is.
