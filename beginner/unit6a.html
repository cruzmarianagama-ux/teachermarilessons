Grammar Bank
Present Simple – He / She / It
✅ Affirmative (Afirmativa)
SubjectVerb + s/esExample
Heplays / goes / has / watchesHe plays tennis.
Sheplays / goes / has / watchesShe goes to the gym.
Itworks / needsIt needs water.
💡 Dica: Com he/she/it, o verbo recebe -s (play → plays), -es (go → goes, watch → watches) ou muda para "has" (have → has) e "does" (do → does).
❌ Negative (Negativa)
SubjectNegativeExample
He / She / Itdoesn't + base verbHe doesn't eat junk food.
💡 Atenção: Depois de doesn't, o verbo volta para a forma base (sem -s): doesn't play, não doesn't plays.
❓ Questions & Short Answers
QuestionShort Answer (Yes)Short Answer (No)
Does he play football?Yes, he does.No, he doesn't.
Does she go to the gym?Yes, she does.No, she doesn't.
💡 Dica: Does vem no início da pergunta, e o verbo principal volta para a forma base: Does he watch...?, não Does he watches...?
