He / She / It · Verb to be (singular) · Short Answers
👤 He / She / It
PronounUsed forPortuguêsExample
Hea man / boyeleMike is from the USA. He is a student.
Shea woman / girlelaEmma is from Canada. She is a student.
Ita thing / animal / foodele / ela (coisa)Sushi is from Japan. It is delicious.
💡 Dica: Usamos he e she para pessoas, e it para coisas, comidas, animais e instrumentos.
🔑 Verb "to be" (singular): am / is / are
SubjectVerbContractionExample
IamI'mI am from Brazil. / I'm from Brazil.
YouareYou'reYou are my teacher. / You're my teacher.
He / She / ItisHe's / She's / It'sShe is from Poland. / She's from Poland.
❓ Yes / No Questions & Short Answers
QuestionShort Answer (Yes)Short Answer (No)
Is he from England?Yes, he is.No, he isn't.
Is she from Australia?Yes, she is.No, she isn't.
Is it from Spain?Yes, it is.No, it isn't.
Are you a student?Yes, I am.No, I'm not.
💡 Atenção: Para formar a pergunta, colocamos Is no início: Is he from England? Não dizemos He is from England?
