Exercise 3 – Writing Challenge: My Usual Day vs. Today
Write 6–8 sentences comparing your normal routine with what is happening today. Include what you usually wear, what you are wearing now, one morning habit, one afternoon habit, what you are doing right now, and at least one sentence with but.
Model text
I usually wear jeans and a T-shirt during the week. Today, I'm wearing black trousers and a sweater. I usually have coffee in the morning and start work early. In the evening, I usually watch TV or read. Right now, I'm working, but I'm having a coffee too. I don't usually wear trainers when I work, but I'm wearing them today.
Useful language: I usually wear... / Today I'm wearing... / Right now I'm... / ... but today ...

Simple Present vs. Present Continuous
Simple Present — Use
UseExample
Habits and routinesShe drinks coffee every morning.
Facts that are always trueHe works in a bank.
With: usually, often, always, every day, neverI don't watch TV at night.
Simple Present — Forms
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
I / you / we / theyworkdon't workDo you work?
he / she / itworksdoesn't workDoes she work?
Short answers: Yes, he does. / No, she doesn't. / Yes, they do. / No, I don't.
Present Continuous — Use
UseExample
Actions happening right nowShe is wearing a dress today.
Temporary situationsHe is working from home this week.
With: now, right now, at the moment, todayI am not watching TV — I'm reading.
Present Continuous — Forms
SubjectAffirmativeNegativeQuestion
Iam wearingam not wearingAm I wearing?
he / she / itis wearingisn't wearingIs she wearing?
you / we / theyare wearingaren't wearingAre they wearing?
Short answers: Yes, she is. / No, he isn't. / Yes, they are. / No, I'm not.
Contrast: Simple Present vs. Present Continuous
Simple PresentPresent Continuous
She usually wears trousers.Today she is wearing a skirt.
He goes to work by bus.Right now he is driving.
They have lunch at work every day.Today they are having lunch at a restaurant.
Key signal words — Simple Present: usually, often, every day, always, never, at the weekend. Present Continuous: now, right now, at the moment, today, this week.
