Adverbs of Frequency · How Often?
⚡ Adverbs of Frequency — Position
AdverbMeaningExample
alwayssempreI always have breakfast.
usuallynormalmenteI usually drink coffee.
oftenfrequentementeI often read the news.
sometimesàs vezesI sometimes go by bus.
hardly everquase nuncaI hardly ever work late.
nevernuncaI never eat fast food.
💡 Position rule: Adverbs of frequency go before the main verb: I always drink coffee. But after the verb "be": She is always late.
📅 How Often? — Expressions
ExpressionMeaningExample
once a weekuma vez/semanaI go to the gym once a week.
twice a weekduas vezes/semanaShe reads twice a week.
three times a weektrês vezes/semanaHe studies three times a week.
once a monthuma vez/mêsI eat fast food once a month.
twice a yearduas vezes/anoWe go on holiday twice a year.
every daytodo diaI have breakfast every day.
💡 Dica: "once" = 1x, "twice" = 2x, "three times" = 3x. A partir de três: número + times: four times a week.
