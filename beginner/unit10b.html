Verb to be: Present Review · Past (was / were)
🔁 Present Review: am / is / are
SubjectVerbExample
IamI am at home.
You / We / TheyareThey are at school.
He / She / ItisShe is at work.
💡 Dica: O passado do verbo to be vem de am/is/are. Lembrar do presente ajuda a entender o passado!
⏳ Past Simple: was / were
SubjectAffirmativeNegativePortuguês
I / He / She / Itwaswasn'tera / estava
You / We / Theywereweren'teram / estavam
💡 Dica: wasn't = was not | weren't = were not.
❓ Questions & Short Answers
QuestionShort Answer (Yes)Short Answer (No)
Was he at work?Yes, he was.No, he wasn't.
Was she at home?Yes, she was.No, she wasn't.
Were they at school?Yes, they were.No, they weren't.
Were you tired?Yes, I was.No, I wasn't.
💡 Atenção: Para formar a pergunta, colocamos Was/Were no início: Where was she? Não dizemos She was where?
