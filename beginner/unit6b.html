How often…? + Transformation
Questions · Write the Question · Sentence Transformation
💡 How often asks about frequency.
❓ How often do you go to the gym? → I go three times a week.
Exercise 1 – Multiple Choice
Choose the correct answer.
🇧🇷 Escolha a resposta correta.
Exercise 2 – Write the Question
Read the answer. Write a "How often…?" question.
🇧🇷 Leia a resposta e escreva a pergunta com "How often".
Gabarito:
1. How often do you go to the gym?
2. How often does Emma have lunch at home?
3. How often do they go shopping?
4. How often does Mike watch TV?
Exercise 3 – Sentence Transformation
Rewrite the sentence so it means the same. Use the word in brackets.
🇧🇷 Reescreva a frase com o mesmo significado usando a palavra entre colchetes.
example I eat fruit every day of the week. (EVERY) → I eat fruit every day.
Gabarito:
1. She never eats meat.
2. He plays football twice a week.
3. They do housework once a week.
4. I usually go to bed at 11:00 pm.

📌 Position Rules
RuleExample
Before main verbI always have breakfast.
After beShe is usually tired.
Frequency expressions → endI go to the gym twice a week.
⚠️ Sometimes can go at the start for emphasis: Sometimes I eat out on Fridays. ✅
❓ How often…?
QuestionShort Answer
How often do you go to the gym?Three times a week.
How often does she watch TV?Every day. / Always.
