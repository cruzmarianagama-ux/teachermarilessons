Pronouns · Be (am/is/are) · Negatives
📌 Pronouns + Be (Pronomes + verbo Be)
PronounBePortuguêsExample
IameuI am Brazilian.
YouarevocêYou are American.
HeiseleHe is Japanese.
SheiselaShe is French.
Itis(coisa/animal)It is new.
WearenósWe are students.
Theyareeles/elasThey are British.
💡 Dica: Use he para homens, she para mulheres, e it para coisas, animais e lugares.
⚠️ Negative Forms (Formas Negativas)
AffirmativeNegativeShort Form
I amI am notI'm not
You areyou are notyou aren't
He/She/It ishe/she/it is nothe/she/it isn't
We/They arewe/they are notwe/they aren't
💡 Dica: "No, it isn't." é uma forma curta e comum de responder negativamente.
❓ Questions with Is/Are
AffirmativeQuestion
You are Brazilian.Are you Brazilian?
She is French.Is she French?
It is open.Is it open?
They are American.Are they American?
💡 Dica: Para fazer perguntas com o verbo be, inverta a ordem: be + sujeito.
🌍 Country → Nationality Pattern
From + Country= Nationality
from BrazilBrazilian
from JapanJapanese
from FranceFrench
from the United StatesAmerican
💡 Dica: "She is from Japan" tem o mesmo sentido que "She is Japanese."
