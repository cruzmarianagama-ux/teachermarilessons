📖 Reading – Your Free Time
Multiple Choice · True/False
Text – Your Free Time
How do 18- to 24-year-olds in the UK spend their free time? A new study shows that a lot of young people stay at home in their free time.

So, what do they do in their free time at home? 55% play computer games and some of them spend time online. A lot of young people also watch films, but not on TV. In fact, 47% of people in the age group never watch TV!

When young people go out, they do different activities. Friends are very important to them and they meet friends for 24% of their free time. The cinema is a favourite place and 72% of 18- to 24-year-olds go to the cinema to watch films. People like different kinds of films – science fiction, comedies, horror – but 47% of them love animation films!

People also do sport or exercise. A lot of young people are healthy because they go for walks or swim, but 40% of teenagers in the UK don't know how to swim!

Young people also travel a lot and they usually buy their tickets online. 41% of all young people go to the beach on their holiday and 40% go on holiday with their family. Young people usually stay in a hotel when they are on holiday.
Exercise 1 – Tick the Correct Option
Read the report and choose the correct option.
🇧🇷 Leia o texto e escolha a opção correta.
Example A lot of 18- to 24-year-olds stay at home ✓ in their free time.
Exercise 2 – True or False
Read the report again and write T (True) or F (False).
🇧🇷 Leia novamente e escreva V (Verdadeiro) ou F (Falso).

🎧 Listening – Weekends & Films
True/False · Multiple Choice
Exercise 1 – Listen and Answer
Listen to an interview where two people talk about their weekends. Write T (True) or F (False).
🇧🇷 Ouça a entrevista sobre o fim de semana das pessoas e escreva V (Verdadeiro) ou F (Falso).
Interviewer: Hello. Can I ask you some questions?
Man: Sure.
Interviewer: Are you married?
Woman: No, he's my brother.
Interviewer: Sorry!
Woman: Don't worry. So what are the questions about?
Interviewer: I want to ask about what you do at the weekend.
Man: Well, I usually work.
Interviewer: Really? What do you do?
Man: I'm a waiter in a restaurant.
Interviewer: So you work late at the weekend?
Man: Yes, and I'm always tired after work, so I usually sleep or watch TV.
Interviewer: And what about you?
Woman: I always go out with my friends on Saturday night.
Interviewer: Where do you go?
Woman: It depends. To the cinema or a restaurant…
Interviewer: The restaurant where your brother works?
Woman: No, that's very expensive.
Interviewer: What's your favourite part of the weekend?
Woman: Sunday mornings…
Interviewer: Why do you like Sunday mornings?
Woman: Because I can sleep late. I never get up before 10 in the morning on Sunday.
Man: Well, good for you!
Interviewer: What time do you usually get up on Sunday?
Man: I usually get up at 8 o'clock.
Interviewer: Do you start work that early?
Man: No, I play football with my friends.

🚀 Reading – My Weekends
True/False · Multiple Choice
Text – My Weekends
Hi! My name is Lucy.

I usually stay at home on Friday evenings because I am tired after work. I watch a film or read a book. My favourite films are comedies and science fiction films. I don't like horror films because I think they are very ugly.

On Saturday morning, I meet my friends. We usually have coffee and then we go for a walk in the park. Sometimes we buy cinema tickets online. We usually watch a film in the afternoon.

My friend Emma loves action films, but I don't like them very much. My brother Ben likes westerns, but I never watch them with him. My parents love animations, so they often watch them together at home.

On Sunday I usually visit my grandparents. We have lunch together and then we relax at home. Sunday is my favourite day of the week because I spend time with my family.
