Grammar Bank
Six question systems in English
1️⃣ Questions with BE (am / is / are)
(Question word)BESubjectExample
–AreyouAre you tired? – Yes, I am. / No, I'm not.
–Isyour sisterIs your sister at work? – No, she isn't.
WhereareyouWhere are you from? – I'm from Brazil.
Whoisthat womanWho is that woman? – She's my mother.
💡 Tip: BE comes before the subject: Where are you from? not Where you are from?
2️⃣ Present Simple Questions (do / does)
(Question word)Do / DoesSubjectBase verb
–Doyou / we / theywork on Saturdays? – Yes, I do.
–Doeshe / she / itdrink coffee? – No, she doesn't.
Wheredoesshework?
How oftendoyougo shopping?
💡 Tip: After does, the verb has no -s: Where does she work? not Where does she works?
3️⃣ BE or DO? How to choose
Is there a main verb?UseExample
No (from, tired, at home, Brazilian…)BEWhere are your parents from?
Yes (work, live, like, have…)DO / DOESWhere do your parents live?
💡 Tip: Look at the answer! Yes, she is → the question uses Is. Yes, she does → the question uses Does.
4️⃣ Questions with CAN
(Question word)CanSubjectBase verb
–Canyouswim? – Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
Whatcanyour sistercook? – Pasta and rice.
WherecanIpark?
💡 Tip: Never use do with can: Can you cook? not Do you can cook?
5️⃣ Questions with WOULD LIKE
UseQuestionAnswer
OfferWould you like some coffee?Yes, please. / No, thanks.
InvitationWould you like to go to the cinema?Yes, I'd love to.
PreferenceWhat would you like to do?I'd like to relax.
💡 Tip: Do you like coffee? = in general. Would you like a coffee? = an offer now.
6️⃣ Present Continuous Questions (now)
(Question word)BESubjectVerb + -ing
Whatareyoudoing right now?
–Isheworking now? – Yes, he is.
Whereisshegoing?
💡 Tip: now / right now → What are you doing? · usually / every day → What do you do?
7️⃣ Questions with THERE IS / THERE ARE
QuestionShort answers
SingularIs there a bank near here?Yes, there is. / No, there isn't.
PluralAre there any cafés near here?Yes, there are. / No, there aren't.
💡 Tip: Never use do / does with there: Is there a gym? not Does there a gym?
