A banker's life

Itsuki Nakamura is a banker. He is from a small town in Japan, but now he lives and works in New York.

He gets up at five in the morning and goes to the gym. Then he goes home, has a shower and makes breakfast. He lives with his wife in a flat in New York, so they have breakfast together. They always have yoghurt and cereal and he usually drinks orange juice.

He goes to work at 8 o'clock and takes the subway to the office. He starts work at 9 o'clock and finishes at 6 o'clock. He sometimes has lunch in the office or at a small café.

Itsuki speaks Japanese, English, French, and Italian. He also studies Spanish.

His wife's name is Isabella and she's American. They don't have children.

Itsuki usually gets home at quarter past seven. He always cooks dinner. Then he reads a book or speaks to his mother on the phone. She lives in Japan.

👪 Possessive adjectives
SubjectPossessiveExample

💡 A possessive adjective goes before a noun: her name, not she's name.
👉 this / these · a / an · plurals
RuleExample
this + singularIs this your key?
these + pluralAre these your sunglasses?
a + consonant sounda book, a purse, a car
an + vowel soundan office, an umbrella, an ID card
plural nouns + -s / -iescity → cities, key → keys
adjectives: no -s, before the nounbig cities (not bigs cities)
