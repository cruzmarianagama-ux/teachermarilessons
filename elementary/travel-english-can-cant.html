Can & Can't – Reference Guide
✅ Positive – Can
SubjectFormExample
I / You / He / She / It / We / Theycan + verbYou can pay by card here.
💡 can does NOT change for he/she/it. Never add -s: She can pay ✔ — She cans pay ✗
❌ Negative – Can't
SubjectFormExample
I / You / He / She / It / We / Theycan't + verbYou can't smoke in here.
💡 can't = cannot. Both are correct. In formal signs, you often see cannot.
❓ Questions – Can I…? / Can you…?
UseFormExample
Ask for permissionCan I + verb?Can I have a window seat?
Ask for helpCan you + verb?Can you help me with my bag?
Ask about a groupCan we + verb?Can we pay separately?
💡 Never say "Can I to have…?" — there is no to after can. The verb is always in its base form.
⚠️ Common Mistakes
IncorrectCorrect
She cans speak English.She can speak English.
Can I to buy a ticket?Can I buy a ticket?
You can't entering here.You can't enter here.
I can't buys food here.I can't buy food here.
Can to you help me?Can you help me?
