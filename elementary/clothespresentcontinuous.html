Grammar Bank
Present Simple · Present Continuous · contrast
📌 Present Simple — routines and habits
SubjectPositiveNegativeQuestion
I / you / we / theyI wear jeans.I don't wear jeans.Do you wear jeans?
he / she / itShe wears jeans.She doesn't wear jeans.Does she wear jeans?
💡 After does / doesn't, use the base verb: Does she wear, not Does she wears.
📌 Present Continuous — happening now
SubjectPositiveNegativeQuestion
II'm wearing a coat.I'm not wearing a coat.Am I wearing a coat?
he / she / itShe's wearing a coat.She isn't wearing a coat.Is she wearing a coat?
you / we / theyThey're wearing coats.They aren't wearing coats.Are they wearing coats?
💡 Use am / is / are + verb-ing. Example: He is working, not He is work.
🔄 Present Simple vs. Present Continuous
Present SimplePresent Continuous
I usually wear sneakers.Today I'm wearing boots.
She works from home.She's working in the office today.
They take the bus every day.They're walking right now.
💡 Usually / every day → routines. Now / right now / at the moment → actions happening now. Today can describe a temporary situation.
