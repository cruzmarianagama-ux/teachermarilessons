Reading & Problem Solving
True or False · Read between the lines · Solve shopping problems
Reading – Shopping in London
Emma is on holiday in London. She needs a new shirt. She goes into a clothes shop.

Sales clerk: Hi! Can I help you?
Emma: Yes, please. I'm looking for a shirt.
Sales clerk: What size are you?
Emma: I'm a medium.
Sales clerk: How about this blue one?
Emma: I like it. Can I try it on?
Sales clerk: Of course. The changing rooms are over there.

Emma tries on the shirt, but it does not fit.

Emma: It's too small. Do you have a large?
Sales clerk: Yes. Here you are.
Emma: Thanks.

The large shirt fits Emma.

Sales clerk: It looks good on you!
Emma: Thanks! How much is it?
Sales clerk: It's £35.
Emma: Great. I'll take it.

Exercise 3A – Your Hotel
You are Chris Miller. Write your lines in the hotel conversation.
Central Hotel | Name: Chris Miller | Reservation: Yes | Room 306
Breakfast: 7:00–10:00 | Lift: on the right
Receptionist: Hello! Welcome to Central Hotel. How can I help you?
You: [Say you have a reservation and give your name]
Receptionist: Great. Can I see your passport or ID, please?
You: [Hand over your document]

You: [Thank them and ask about breakfast time]
Receptionist: We serve breakfast between 7:00 and 10:00.
You: [Ask where the lift is]
Receptionist: It's on your right.
You: [Say thank you]
Exercise 3B – Your Shopping Trip
Now you go shopping. Write your lines in the conversation.
T-shirt | Size: M | Not green | Try it on: Yes | Max price: £30 | Payment: credit card
Sales clerk: Hi! Can I help you?
You: [Say what you are looking for]
Sales clerk: Sure. What size are you?
You: [Give your size]
Sales clerk: How about this green one?
You: [Ask what colours it comes in]
Sales clerk: It comes in blue, black and green.
You: [Ask to see the blue one]
Sales clerk: Sure. Here's a blue one.
You: [Ask to try it on]
Sales clerk: Of course. The changing rooms are over there.
(You try it on.)
Sales clerk: Do you like it?
You: [Say yes and ask the price]
Sales clerk: It's £28.
You: [Say you'll take it]
Sales clerk: Great. Are you paying in cash?
You: [Say no — credit card]
