One Day on Holiday

Exercise 1 – Choose what you need to say
You're on holiday. Choose the best thing to say in each situation. Item 1 is the example.
Exercise 2 – Read the travel diary
Read and answer the questions. Write short answers. Item 1 is the example.
My first day in Edinburgh

I arrived at my hotel at 2:00. I had a reservation, so check-in was easy. My room was 407, on the fourth floor. The receptionist gave me my key card and told me the lift was on the left.

The hotel had free Wi-Fi, a gym and a pool. There was no balcony in my room. Breakfast was from 7:00 to 10:00.

In the afternoon, I went shopping. I found a beautiful green shirt. I normally wear a medium, but the medium didn't fit, so I tried a large. It was perfect. The shirt was £29. I paid by credit card.
