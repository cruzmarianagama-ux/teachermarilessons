Possessive 's – Rules
RuleExample
Singular noun → add 'sMy brother's car is fast.
Plural noun ending in -s → add 'My parents' house is big.
Irregular plural → add 'sThe children's toys are on the floor.
The possessive 's is NOT the same as a plural -s. "My parents live here" (plural) vs "my parents' car" (both parents' car).
