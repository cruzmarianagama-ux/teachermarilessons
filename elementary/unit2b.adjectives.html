Adjective rules & useful patterns
Adjective Position
PositionPatternExample
Before nounadjective + nouna new phone
After besubject + be + adjectiveThe phone is new.
Both positions are correct in English. "A new phone" and "The phone is new" mean the same thing.
Giving Opinions – I think...
PatternExample
I think + subject + be + adjective.I think the hotel is good.
I think + subject + be + adjective.I think the road is dangerous.
Use I think... to give your opinion. It is NOT a fact – it is what YOU believe.
Fact vs Opinion
TypeSignalExample
Factno signal wordThe car is red.
OpinionI think...I think the car is beautiful.
Contrasting with BUT
PatternExample
adjective, but adjectiveThe room is small, but clean.
adjective, but adjectiveThe building is old, but beautiful.
Use but to connect two contrasting ideas about the same thing.
