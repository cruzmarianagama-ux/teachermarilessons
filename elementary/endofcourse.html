Be or Do? Present or Past?
Be vs Do/Does/Did in questions · Present & past of be · Error correction · Mixed forms
Grammar Tips for Day 1 Questions with BE: Are you tired? · Is she a doctor? · Were they late? → Use be when the verb IS be.
Questions with DO/DOES/DID: Do you work here? · Does she teach? · Did they go? → Use do/does/did with all other verbs.
Common mistakes: ✗ Do you are...? ✗ Does she is...? ✗ Did he was...? — These are ALWAYS wrong. Be never uses do/does/did.
Exercise 1 – Be or Do / Does / Did?
Choose the correct question word. Think carefully: is the verb BE or another verb?
Example (done for you)Is she a teacher? (verb = be → use IS, not Does) ✔
Exercise 2 – Present or Past? Choose the Correct Form of Be
Read the whole sentence carefully. Choose the correct form of be — present (am/is/are) or past (was/were).
Example (done for you)The concert was amazing last night. (last night → past) ✔
Exercise 3 – Find and Fix the Mistake
Each sentence has one mistake. Write the correct sentence in the box. Look at the example first.
Example (done for you) Wrong: Do you are a student?
Correct: Are you a student? — We never use DO with the verb BE.
Exercise 4 – Complete the Conversation
Click a word from the box, then click the gap to complete the conversation. Pay attention to the verb and the time reference in each line.
Example (done for you)Ana: Are you from Spain?  Ben: No, I'm from Portugal. → verb = be, present → ARE ✔

Daily Life
Present simple (+/–) · Verb phrases · Adverbs of frequency · Writing
Grammar Tips for Day 3Present simple: I/you/we/they work · he/she/it works (add –s/–es/–ies).
Negative: I don't work · She doesn't work (base verb, no –s!).
Adverbs of frequency: always → usually → often → sometimes → rarely → never. Go BEFORE the main verb: I always have breakfast. But AFTER be: She is often late.

The Past
Was / Were · Past simple regular & irregular · Time expressions · Writing
Grammar Tips for Day 5Was/Were: I/he/she/it was · you/we/they were. Negative: wasn't / weren't.
Regular past: walk→walked · study→studied · stop→stopped.
Irregular: go→went · have→had · see→saw · eat→ate · write→wrote.
Negative/Question: I didn't go. Did you go? (NOT did you went).

Food & Quantities
Countable / Uncountable · a/an/some/any · How much/many · Food containers
Grammar Tips for Day 6Countable: an egg · two eggs.  Uncountable: some milk (NOT a milk / two milks).
Some/Any: I have some bread (+) · I don't have any sugar (–) · Do you have any eggs? (?)
How much/many: How much rice? (uncountable) · How many tomatoes? (countable).
Containers: a bottle of oil · a carton of milk · a jar of jam · a packet of pasta.

Adverbs & Verb Patterns
Adverbs of manner · want/need/would like + to · Articles the / no article
Grammar Tips for Day 8Adverbs of manner: adjective + -ly: slow→slowly · careful→carefully. Irregular: good→well · fast→fast · hard→hard.
Verb + to + infinitive: I want to go. She needs to study. We would like to eat.
The: Use the for unique things (the sun, the internet) and with superlatives. No article for general ideas: I like music. Music is great.

Experiences & Stories
Present perfect · Past simple vs present perfect · Past participles · Writing
Grammar Tips for Day 9Present perfect: have/has + past participle. Use for EXPERIENCES (no specific time): I have seen that film. She has never been to Paris.
Past simple: Use when you say WHEN: I saw it last week. She went to Paris in 2019.
ever/never: Have you ever eaten sushi? I've never tried it.  already/yet: I've already eaten. I haven't eaten yet.

This year, Sydney, Australia was voted the best city in the world. I'm Francesca Williams, from Melbourne, where I've lived all my life. I'd like to tell you why I think Melbourne is better than Sydney.
