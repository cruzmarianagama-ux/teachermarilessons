Exercise 1 – The Wrong Train
Read the story and choose the correct answer.
On Friday, Olivia finished work at 5:30 and took a train to Brighton to visit her friend Chloe. She was tired and didn't look carefully at the information on the platform. After about twenty minutes, she looked out of the window and saw a station she didn't know.

Olivia asked another passenger, "Does this train go to Brighton?" The man smiled and said, "No. This train goes to Bristol."

Olivia got off at the next station and called Chloe. She waited forty minutes for another train and finally arrived in Brighton at 9:15. Chloe met her at the station. They were hungry, so they went to a small restaurant near Chloe's flat.

Exercise 3 – Writing Challenge: A Short Story From Your Past
Write 6–8 sentences about a memorable day, weekend, trip or important moment from your past.
  • ✓ Say when it happened.
  • ✓ Say where you were or went.
  • ✓ Say who was with you.
  • ✓ Use at least three Past Simple affirmative verbs.
  • ✓ Use at least one irregular verb.
  • ✓ Use at least one negative sentence.
  • ✓ Say how the experience was.
Model text
Last year, I went to São Paulo for a weekend with two friends. We stayed at a small hotel near the city centre. On Saturday, we visited a museum and had lunch at a nice restaurant. In the afternoon, I bought some presents for my family. We didn't go out at night because we were very tired. On Sunday, we had breakfast at a café and walked around the city. We came home in the evening. It was a great weekend.
