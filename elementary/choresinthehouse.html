Like / Hate + -ing
PositiveNegative
I like cooking.I hate cleaning the bathroom.
She loves walking the dog.He doesn't like vacuuming.
After like, love, hate, don't mind — use verb + -ing: I like cooking. I hate cleaning.
Have to
SubjectFormExample
I / you / we / theyhave to + verbI have to take out the rubbish.
he / she / ithas to + verbShe has to wash the clothes.
Have to means it is necessary. After have to / has to, use the base verb (no -ing, no -s).
Can / Can't
UseExample
Offer to do somethingI can vacuum the living room.
Say you are not ableI can't walk the dog because I'm at work.
After can / can't, always use the base verb: I can cook. I can't clean. Never: I can to cook.
Can you…? (Requests)
RequestPositive replyNegative reply
Can you cook dinner?Sure, no problem.Sorry, I can't because I'm busy.
