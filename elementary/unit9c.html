Emma's Weekend Trip

Last weekend, Emma visited two cities: Brighton and Oxford.

Brighton is smaller than Oxford, but Emma thinks it is more exciting. The beach is beautiful, and there are many cafés and small shops.

Oxford is older than Brighton. It has many famous universities and museums. Emma thinks the museums are more interesting than the shops in Brighton.

Hotels in Oxford are more expensive than hotels in Brighton, but the restaurants are better. Emma says the food in Oxford is delicious.

Brighton is warmer than Oxford in the spring, but Oxford is quieter. Emma likes both cities, but she wants to visit Brighton again because she loves the beach.
Exercise 1 – Choose the Correct Answer
Choose the correct answer based on the text.
Exercise 2 – True or False
Read each statement and decide: True (T) or False (F)?
Exercise 3 – Complete the Sentences
Use information from the text to complete each sentence.
Exercise 4 – Answer the Questions
Write complete sentences.
1. Which city is older?
2. Which city has better restaurants?
3. Which city is warmer in the spring?
4. Why does Emma want to visit Brighton again?
5. Which city would you like to visit? Why?

PatternExample
