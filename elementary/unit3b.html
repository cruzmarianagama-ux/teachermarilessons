Present Simple (+/–/?) · Talking About Jobs
📌 Present Simple (Affirmative)
SubjectVerbExample
I / you / we / theyverb (base form)I live in a flat.
he / she / itverb + s/esShe works in an office.
💡 Tip: With he/she/it, the verb takes -s or -es: study → studies, have → has, do → does.
🔑 Present Simple (Negative)
SubjectNegativeExample
I / you / we / theydon't + verbI don't play tennis.
he / she / itdoesn't + verbIt doesn't rain a lot here.
❓ Present Simple (Questions)
Question wordAuxiliaryExample
–Do / DoesDo you have a dog?
Whatdo / doesWhat do you do?
Wheredo / doesWhere does she work?
–Is / AreIs she a doctor? / Are they pilots?
💡 Tip: With be, we don't use do/does: Is he a builder? not "Does he be a builder?".

PatternExample

I work for + companyI work for an IT company.
I work in + placeI work in a shop. / I work in digital marketing.
I work part-time / from homeI work part-time. / I work from home.
