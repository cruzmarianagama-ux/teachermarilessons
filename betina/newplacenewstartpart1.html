Asking Questions
StructureExample
Are there any + noun?Are there any extra fees?
Is there a + noun?Is there a monthly fee?
How much is + noun?How much is the rent?
How much are + noun (plural)?How much are the bills?
When will I + verb?When will I receive my card?
Can I + verb?Can I use my debit card online?
Do I need to + verb?Do I need to pay a deposit?
Notice: Is there a (singular) vs Are there any (plural/uncountable). How much is (singular) vs How much are (plural).

AdjectiveComparativeExample
