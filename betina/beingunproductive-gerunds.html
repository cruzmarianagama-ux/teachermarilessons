Grammar Bank
Uses of gerund
Gerunds as subjects
Use the -ing form as the subject of a sentence.
Walking helps me relax.
Creating something is enjoyable.
After certain verbs
enjoy / consider / do not mind / cannot stand / cannot imagine / practise + verb-ing
She enjoys painting.
We considered taking a break.
After phrasal verbs
give up / take up / put off + verb-ing
He gave up working on Sundays.
She took up drawing.
After prepositions
by / without / about / in / at / of + verb-ing
You can relax by walking.
She is interested in painting.
Look forward to + gerund
In look forward to, to is a preposition.
I look forward to meeting my friends.
Spelling reminders
make → making · take → taking · receive → receiving
sit → sitting · plan → planning
play → playing · study → studying
